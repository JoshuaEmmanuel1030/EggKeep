**Verdict**

The brief can keep the hypothesis, but it must inherit the findings below rather than the concept's wording.

**Strongest counter-argument**

The hypothesis merges two different records into one irreversible action. The sheet's warehouse columns are sparse and asymmetric (Butir OK on 65 of 108 return rows, Tgl Barang Balik on 81, Aryo ref on 43 of 59); the "OK = remainder" rule is inferred from Osave rows. Making admin's transcription of that paper the stock movement, with no undo, no provenance, outflow linkage by text invoice ref, and a second path on old phone bundles writing to the same outflows, converts every typo and every unverified remainder into a stock delta that only the owner can reverse by SQL. The full spec's own §5.2 shape is safer and barely bigger: the line is paper (editable, no stock) and "Catat barang balik" is a separate, explicit stock save.

**Simpler alternatives**

A. Reuse `RecordReturnDialog` from the delivery line, prefilled (pecah into Retak/Hancur, OK remainder shown, locked to that SKU, `deliveryLineId` prop emitted on every line). Gives up the one-form feel; keeps caps, shared-egg budget, kg rounding, box resolution and their tests, and avoids a third copy of `skuMap` (already duplicated in the dialog and `GroupedActivityLog.tsx` lines 1061–1072). The PO's egg logs must be fetched either way.

B. `delivery_lines` minimal: `sku_code, qty_returned_unit, reason, claim_ref, note, expected_expiry_override, source, import_key` plus audit fields; everything warehouse-side derived from `returns`. Gives up nothing material.

C. Import store-side lines and Aryo refs only (as full spec Phase 4 says) and do not import October's warehouse counts as returns; fix stock with the existing stock count instead. Gives up October's returns in EggKeep stock history; avoids restocking ~21,000 pieces into consumed October batches from numbers the sheet leaves blank on 40% of rows, against stock that physical counts already say is wrong.
